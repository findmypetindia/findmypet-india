Found Cats in Chennai

Found a cat in Chennai? Capture the location, time and visible features safely so a possible owner can compare the report with their missing cat.

Report a Found CatCheck AI Search

Location details matter

Record Chennai plus the exact locality, street, apartment area or nearby landmark, along with the date and approximate time. Cats may be searched for close to the place where they were last seen.

Take useful photos and notes

  • Capture the face, body and coat pattern when safe.
  • Record colour, eye colour, approximate size and visible markings.
  • Note distinctive ear, tail, patch or scar features.
  • Record collar or harness details if present.
  • If breed is uncertain, describe the visible appearance rather than guessing.

Keep one verification detail private

A public report does not need to reveal every identifying feature. Keeping one less-obvious detail private can help distinguish a genuine owner from someone relying only on the public photo.

Verify ownership before handover

Ask a claimant for older photos, veterinary or vaccination information, or knowledge of a distinctive feature. Do not rely on visual similarity alone, and never share OTPs or send money simply because someone claims the cat is theirs.

AI-assisted photo search

FindMyPet India's AI Search can surface possible visual similarities between found and lost pet reports. Use those results as leads and cross-check locality, dates, markings and independent ownership evidence.

Frequently asked questions

What if the cat is frightened?

Prioritise safe observation and avoid unnecessary restraint. Photograph from a safe distance when possible.

Should I guess the breed?

No. Visible colour, pattern, size and markings are more useful than an uncertain breed label.

What if the cat appears injured?

Seek appropriate veterinary or animal-welfare assistance promptly and do not rely only on an online listing.
