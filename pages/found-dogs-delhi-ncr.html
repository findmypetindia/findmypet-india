Found Dogs in Delhi NCR

If you found a dog in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad or Ghaziabad, create a detailed report with the exact location, time and a clear photo.

Report Found DogSearch by Photo

What to include

  1. Take a clear photo without putting the animal at risk.
  2. Enter the correct NCR city and exact sector, colony, road or landmark.
  3. Record when and where the dog was found and note collar or distinctive marks.
  4. Keep public information focused on identification and avoid unnecessary private details.

Help the owner recognise the dog

Describe visible features accurately. If someone claims the dog, ask for reasonable proof such as older photographs, identifying marks or vaccination information before handover.

Check lost-dog reports

Compare your found dog's photo and location with nearby lost reports. Coat pattern and unique markings can be more useful than breed alone.
