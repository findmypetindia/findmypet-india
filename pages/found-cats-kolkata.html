Found Cats in Kolkata

For a found cat in Kolkata, record the exact locality, nearby landmark, date, time and a clear photo. Avoid exposing every unique identifying detail.

Report Found CatAI Search

What to record

Location
Neighbourhood, street, building, park or landmark.
Time
When and where the cat was found or first noticed.
Appearance
Coat pattern, colour, eye colour, sex, size and visible marks.
Photo
Clear face, ears and coat-pattern photos when safe.

Keep the cat calm and safe

Cats may hide or become stressed in unfamiliar surroundings. Give the cat a quiet, secure space if you can safely do so, and seek veterinary or rescue help when appropriate.

Verify the claimant

Keep one or two distinctive identifiers private. Ask a claimant for older photos or matching information before handover, and use a safe meeting arrangement.

Privacy and sharing

Share the report link and clear photo without unnecessary personal information. Update the report when the cat is reunited or moved to a safer location.

FAQs

Why keep an identifier private?

It gives you a useful way to check whether a claimant genuinely knows the cat.

Can I search by photo?

Yes. Use FindMyPet India's AI Search with a clear image to help compare available reports.

What if the cat is hiding nearby?

Record the exact location and ask nearby residents, security staff and local feeders to watch for the cat.
