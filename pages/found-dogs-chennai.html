Found Dogs in Chennai

Found a dog in Chennai? Record where and when you found the dog, take safe clear photos and create a report that helps a possible owner recognise the animal.

Report a Found DogCheck AI Search

Record the exact location

Use Chennai plus the specific locality, road, apartment area or landmark. Add the date and approximate time. A precise location can help an owner compare the report with where the dog was last seen.

Photograph and describe what you can see

  • Take a clear face photo and, when safe, a full-body photo.
  • Record coat colour, approximate size and visible markings.
  • Note collar or harness details without assuming ownership.
  • Mention distinctive ears, tail, patches or scars that are actually visible.
  • If breed is uncertain, describe appearance instead of guessing.

Help owners search safely

Keep the report factual and avoid publishing every identifying detail. You can retain one less-obvious feature so a claimant can demonstrate genuine knowledge of the dog. Share the report through appropriate local channels as well as FindMyPet India.

Verify before handover

A visual match alone is not enough. Ask a possible owner for older photographs, vaccination or veterinary information, or a private identifying detail. Avoid OTPs, bank details and requests for advance payments.

AI-assisted matching

AI Search can help surface possible similarities between a found dog's photo and existing reports. Treat it as a discovery aid and verify location, date, markings and ownership evidence independently.

Frequently asked questions

What if I cannot identify the breed?

Do not guess. Use visible colour, size, coat and markings.

Should I share my personal number publicly?

Share only what is necessary and use the platform's available contact flow where appropriate.

What should I do if the dog is injured?

Prioritise safe veterinary or animal-welfare assistance rather than relying only on an online report.
