Using the platform

FindMyPet India provides online tools for sharing lost and found pet reports, reviewing possible matches, submitting animal-rescue information and applying to join the rescue-partner network. Use the website lawfully, respectfully and only for genuine animal-help purposes.

Report accuracy and permission

You are responsible for the accuracy of the details, photographs, videos and contact information you submit. Upload only material you have the right to share. Do not post false, misleading, abusive, unlawful, discriminatory or unrelated content, or another person's private information without permission.

Public reports and communication

Information submitted to community features may be displayed to help people identify an animal and contact the person who submitted a report. Do not include sensitive information that is not needed for that purpose. Communicate respectfully and report suspected misuse to us.

AI-assisted matching

Photo comparison, automated suggestions and community information are intended to help users review possible matches. They may be incomplete or inaccurate and do not confirm an animal's identity, ownership or reunion. Users must independently verify any possible match.

Animal rescue reports and emergency limitations

FindMyPet India is an information and reporting platform, not an emergency-response service, veterinary provider or guarantee of rescue. Submitting a report does not mean that an organisation has received it, accepted the case or will arrive. Where an animal or person is in immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency, veterinary or animal-welfare services directly and follow qualified safety guidance.

Rescue partners and listings

Partner applications may be reviewed before a listing is published. A directory listing or application status is not a guarantee of a partner's availability, qualifications, response time, service quality or outcome. Confirm services, operating hours, charges and suitability directly with the organisation or rescuer before relying on them.

Safety and ownership verification

Meet and communicate safely. Verify pet ownership before arranging a handover. Do not share passwords, OTPs, bank credentials or advance payments with people you do not trust. Do not put yourself or an animal at risk while attempting a rescue.

Content moderation and access

We may review, restrict or remove reports, partner listings or accounts where content appears unsafe, misleading, unlawful, abusive, fraudulent or inconsistent with these terms. We may update, suspend or discontinue features where reasonably necessary to operate or protect the platform.
