What to include in a found-dog report

  • Clear photos of the dog's face, body and distinctive markings.
  • Exact Ajmer neighbourhood, road or landmark where it was found.
  • Date and approximate time found.
  • Colour, breed or likely breed, collar and visible identifying features.

Help the owner verify the dog

Do not publish every unique identifying detail if doing so could make false claims easier. Ask a claimant for reasonable proof such as older photographs, distinctive marks or vaccination information before arranging a handover.
