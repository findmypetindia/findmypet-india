Found Dogs in Bengaluru

Found a dog in Bengaluru? A clear photo, accurate locality and useful identifying details can make it easier for the family to recognise the dog and respond quickly.

What to include in a found-dog report

  • Use a recent, clear photo showing the dog's face, ears, coat and body markings.
  • Record the date and approximate time when you found the dog.
  • Add the Bengaluru area, street, apartment/community name or a nearby landmark where it was found.
  • Describe breed or appearance, coat colour, gender if known, collar/tags and distinctive marks.
  • If the dog is being temporarily kept safe, mention only practical, non-sensitive information needed for identification.

How to describe the location

Use the most specific safe location you can provide—for example, a neighbourhood plus a nearby public landmark. If the exact spot is unsafe to publish, give a nearby landmark rather than exposing a private residential address.

For Bengaluru, details such as the locality, main road, metro or bus landmark, park, market or other public reference point can help an owner recognise the area.

Help identify the right dog

Photos are especially useful when they show distinctive features such as ear shape, muzzle, coat pattern, tail, patches, scars or a collar. Compare more than one feature rather than relying only on breed or colour.

You can also use FindMyPet India's AI Search to assist with photo-based searching. Treat matches as leads and verify distinctive details before assuming a report is the same dog.

Safety & privacy

Do not publish unnecessary phone numbers, home addresses, passwords or other private information. If you have found a dog, prioritise safe handling and appropriate local veterinary or animal-rescue help when the dog is injured or distressed.

Found dog FAQs

What should I do first after finding a dog in Bengaluru?

Move the dog to a safe place only if you can do so safely, note the location and time, take a clear photo and check for visible identification such as a collar or tag.

Should I include the exact address where I found the dog?

Prefer a safe public landmark or locality. Avoid publishing a private residential address unless it is genuinely necessary.

Can a photo help find the owner?

Yes. A clear photo showing distinctive facial, coat and body features can help owners compare the found dog with their missing-pet reports. AI Search can provide an additional lead, but visual details should still be verified.
