What Affects Rooftop Solar Cost?

Solar pricing is not one fixed amount for every property. It can depend on system capacity (kW), panel and inverter specifications, mounting structure, roof condition, wiring, net-metering requirements, installation scope and the city or state. Submit your enquiry for a location-specific discussion instead of relying on a generic online estimate.

PM Surya Ghar Scheme & Solar Subsidy Information

Eligible residential consumers may be able to apply under the Government of India's PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, subject to current scheme rules, technical requirements and the official application process.
