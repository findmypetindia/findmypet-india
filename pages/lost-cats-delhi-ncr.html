Lost Cats in Delhi NCR

If your cat is missing in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad or Ghaziabad, report the last-seen location and a clear photo so local people can help.

Report Lost CatSearch by Photo

Make the lost cat report useful

  1. Use a recent photo showing the face, coat pattern and distinctive features.
  2. Enter the correct NCR city and add the locality, sector, colony, road or landmark.
  3. Include last-seen date/time, collar information and identifying marks.
  4. Share one consistent report link with nearby residents, feeders, vets and rescuers.

Search carefully around the last-seen area

Cats may hide close to familiar territory. Check quiet lanes, building corners, parking areas, gardens and sheltered spaces, then widen the search gradually.

Compare found-cat reports

Check nearby found-pet reports and compare the actual coat pattern, facial markings and other distinctive features instead of relying only on colour or breed.
