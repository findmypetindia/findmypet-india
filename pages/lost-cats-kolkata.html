Lost Cats in Kolkata

For a missing cat, record the exact locality, last-seen time, nearby landmark and a clear recent photo. Indoor cats may stay hidden close to home.

Report Lost CatAI Search

What to include

Exact area
Neighbourhood, street, building, park or nearby landmark.
Last seen
Date, approximate time and where the cat was heading.
Identification
Coat pattern, colour, eye colour, sex, size and distinctive marks.
Photo
Use a clear image showing the face, ears and coat pattern.

Search close to home first

For indoor cats, check stairwells, rooftops, parking areas, storage spaces and quiet hiding places around the building. For outdoor cats, expand gradually through familiar routes, feeders, parks and nearby lanes.

Update credible sightings

Record the exact place and time of each credible sighting. Keeping the report current helps people search the most likely movement area.

Safety and privacy

Do not publish unnecessary personal information or every unique identifier. Verify sightings carefully and arrange recovery safely.

FAQs

Should I search nearby buildings?

Yes. Cats can remain hidden surprisingly close to the escape point, especially when frightened.

Can I use AI Search?

Yes. Upload a clear pet photo through FindMyPet India's AI Search to help compare available reports.

What if the cat is sighted in another locality?

Add the new sighting, date, time and location to the report and adjust the search area.
