Found Cats in Mumbai

If you find a cat in Mumbai, record where and when you found it and use a clear photo so people searching for a missing cat can compare the report.

Report Found CatAI Pet Search

Record the exact location

Mention the Mumbai locality, building or society, nearby landmark and approximate time found. Location details are especially useful when matching a found-cat report with a missing-pet report.

Photograph the cat clearly

Use a clear photo showing the face, coat pattern and visible markings. Do not expose unnecessary personal information in the report.

Confirm ownership carefully

Before handover, ask the claimant for older photographs or private identifying details that were not included in the public report. Confirm the match before releasing the cat.
