Lost Cats in Jaipur, Rajasthan

Searching for a missing cat in Jaipur? Create a detailed lost-cat report with a clear photo, last-seen location and identifying features to support a local search.

Report a Lost CatReport a Found Cat

What to include in a lost-cat report

  • Recent photos showing the face, coat pattern and body.
  • Jaipur neighbourhood, road or landmark and last-seen date/time.
  • Colour, breed, eye colour, collar and distinctive marks.
  • Whether the cat is indoor-only, used to roam, shy or approachable.

Search carefully around the last-seen location

Cats can hide close to home. Check quiet corners, parked vehicles, stairwells, gardens, sheds and familiar routes, and ask neighbours and local feeders to watch for the cat.

Check found-pet reports

Compare photos and identifying marks with found-cat reports. Confirm ownership carefully before arranging a handover.
