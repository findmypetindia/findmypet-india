About FindMyPet India

FindMyPet India is a community-driven platform dedicated to helping lost pets return home safely. We connect pet parents, animal lovers, NGOs and rescuers across India through technology.

❤️ Our Story

Lost-pet cases can be difficult to coordinate when information is scattered across social media and local groups. FindMyPet India provides a dedicated place for people to submit lost and found pet reports and review possible matches shared by the community.

🎯 Our Mission

To make lost and found pet information easier to share and review, while encouraging safe communication between pet owners, finders and animal helpers.

👁️ Our Vision

To build a useful, responsible community resource for lost and found pets and support access to animal-welfare information.
