About FindMyPet India

FindMyPet India is a community-driven platform dedicated to helping lost pets return home safely. We connect pet parents, animal lovers, NGOs and rescuers across India through technology.

❤️ Our Story

Every year thousands of pets go missing across India. Many families never find them simply because there is no single trusted platform where people can report lost and found pets. FindMyPet India was created to solve this problem by bringing pet owners, rescuers, volunteers and animal NGOs together.

🎯 Our Mission

To reunite every lost pet with its family using technology, AI-powered search and the power of community.

👁️ Our Vision

To become India's largest and most trusted Lost & Found Pet network while supporting animal welfare organisations and responsible pet ownership.
