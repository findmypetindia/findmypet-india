About FindMyPet India

FindMyPet India is a community-driven platform dedicated to helping lost pets return home safely. We connect pet parents, animal lovers, NGOs and rescuers across India through technology.

❤️ Our Story

Lost-pet cases can be difficult to coordinate when information is scattered across social media and local groups. FindMyPet India provides a dedicated place for people to submit lost and found pet reports and review possible matches shared by the community.

🎯 Our Mission

To make lost and found pet information easier to share and review, while encouraging safe communication between pet owners, finders and animal helpers.

👁️ Our Vision

To build a useful, responsible community resource for lost and found pets and support access to animal-welfare information.

🔎 Share a clear report

Pet parents and finders can submit lost or found pet details, including a recent photo, location, date and identifying features.

🤝 Review possible matches

Community members can compare report details and contact the relevant submitter. AI-assisted results are suggestions only and do not confirm an animal’s identity.

🛡️ Use safe communication

Verify details before arranging a meeting. Choose a public place, take someone with you where possible, and never share OTPs or financial passwords.

📝 What information makes a report useful?

A report is easier for others to understand when it includes a recent, clear photograph, the pet's visible colour and markings, the date, and the last-seen or found location. Add only details you know. If the breed, age or gender is uncertain, say so rather than guessing.

🔍 How people can use the platform

A pet parent or finder can create a Lost or Found report. Visitors can learn about the reporting process and registered users can access the platform's report features. People reviewing a possible match should compare identifying details and contact the report submitter to verify it.

🤖 What AI-assisted search can and cannot do

AI-assisted photo comparison can help surface potentially similar reports. It does not identify an animal with certainty, prove ownership, track a pet's live location or replace a microchip. Always verify a possible match using multiple identifying details.

🔐 Privacy and responsible sharing

Share only the information needed to help identify and locate the pet. Do not publish passwords, OTPs, banking details or other sensitive information. Before meeting anyone, verify their claim, choose a public place and consider taking another person with you.

⚠️ Important service limitations

FindMyPet India is an online reporting and information platform. It does not guarantee that a missing pet will be found, independently verify every user-submitted report, provide a live GPS tracking service or operate as an emergency ambulance or rescue helpline. For an injured animal or immediate danger, contact an appropriate local animal-welfare or emergency service.

📩 Questions or corrections?

If you have a question about the website, need to report a technical issue or want to request a correction, please use our Contact Us page. For information about personal-data handling, read our Privacy Policy.
