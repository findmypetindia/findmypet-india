What to include in a lost-cat report

  • Recent, clear photos from useful angles
  • Cat's name, breed, color, coat pattern and distinctive markings
  • Last-seen date, approximate time, area and nearby landmark
  • Whether the cat is indoor-only, outdoor, shy or likely to approach people
  • Other identifying details such as collar, microchip information or unique features

How to search around Hyderabad

Start with the last-seen location and nearby streets, apartment communities, parks and familiar hiding places. Recheck reports regularly because a cat may be found after the original report is published.

Use a recent photo and distinctive markings when comparing possible matches. Avoid publicly posting a personal phone number; use your FindMyPet India account/contact flow where available.

Before reporting a possible match

Compare multiple identifying features rather than relying only on coat color. If the cat is frightened or injured, approach calmly and consider contacting an appropriate local animal-care professional instead of chasing it.
