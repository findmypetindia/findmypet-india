Found Cats in Navi Mumbai

Found a cat in Navi Mumbai? Record the exact node, sector, landmark, date and time and use clear photos of the face, ears and coat pattern to help the owner recognise the cat.

Report Found CatUse AI Search

Record the exact location

Include node, sector and a nearby building, road, park, station, market or other landmark. Note when and where the cat was seen and update the report if it moves.

Photograph identifying features

Use clear photos of the face, ears, eyes, coat pattern, tail and distinctive markings. Keep at least one unique identifying detail private when practical.

Approach carefully

Cats can be frightened or defensive in unfamiliar surroundings. Avoid chasing or cornering the cat. If safe, provide a quiet sheltered place and ask a local vet, feeder or rescuer for assistance.

Verify ownership before handover

Ask a claimant for older photos, veterinary or vaccination information, or another private identifying detail. A public description alone should not be treated as proof of ownership.

Use AI Search as supporting evidence

Photo matching can help surface possible matches, but it is not definitive. Confirm identity using several visual features and reliable ownership evidence.

FAQs

What makes a found-cat report useful?

Exact node and sector, landmark, date, time and clear photos of distinctive features.

Should I publish every identifying detail?

No. Keeping one or two unique details private can help verify a genuine claimant.

Can I use AI Search to find the owner?

It can help identify visual matches, but the final identity should be confirmed independently.
