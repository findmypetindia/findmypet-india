What to record when you find a cat

📍 Exact location

Note the Bengaluru locality, street or apartment area, nearby landmark, and the place where the cat was first seen.

📸 Clear photos

Take well-lit photos showing the cat's face, ears, coat pattern, tail and any distinctive markings. Avoid heavily filtered images.

🐱 Identifying details

Record approximate age, coat colour, pattern, breed type if known, sex if confidently identified, collar or tag, and unusual markings.

🕒 Date & time

Include when and where you found the cat. This helps an owner compare the report with when their cat went missing.

Useful areas to check around Bengaluru

If the cat is calm and safe, check nearby entrances, parking areas, gardens, apartment common areas and familiar-looking routes. Do not chase a frightened cat into traffic. A cat may hide nearby, so preserve the original location and time details in your report.

How to identify a found cat

  • Compare the face shape, eye colour, ear shape and coat pattern.
  • Look for collars, tags, microchip information or distinctive accessories if safely visible.
  • Record scars, patches, clipped ears or other unique markings.
  • Use more than one clear photo when possible.
  • If you are unsure about breed or sex, describe observable features rather than guessing.

FAQs

What should I do first after finding a cat in Bengaluru?

Move only if necessary for immediate safety. Record the original location, take clear photos, and report the cat with accurate identifying details.

Should I guess the cat's breed or sex?

No. If you are uncertain, describe observable features such as coat colour, pattern and markings instead of guessing.

Can AI Search confirm that this is someone's cat?

No. AI Search can help surface possible matches, but ownership should be confirmed using reliable identifying information.
