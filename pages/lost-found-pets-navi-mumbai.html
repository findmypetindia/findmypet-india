Record the Navi Mumbai node, sector and landmark

Navi Mumbai covers multiple nodes and neighbourhoods. A useful report should name the actual node—such as Vashi, Nerul, Seawoods, CBD Belapur, Kharghar, Airoli, Ghansoli or Panvel—then add the sector, road, building entrance or nearby public landmark. These are examples only; choose the location that matches the report. Include the date and approximate time, and distinguish a confirmed sighting from an unverified message.

If a pet is seen near a station, highway, creek, construction area or other hazardous location, do not enter restricted or unsafe spaces. Record the nearest safe public landmark and ask local people who know the area to share the report.

Make updates easy for nearby people to follow

  • Use one recent photo that clearly shows the pet's face and body.
  • Describe coat colour, size, collar and distinctive markings.
  • Keep one or two less-visible identifiers private for ownership checks.
  • When a new sighting arrives, record its node, sector, date and time separately.

Before a found pet is handed over, ask the claimant for older photographs or veterinary information. FindMyPet India helps people share reports and review possible matches; it does not confirm identity or guarantee that a rescue team is available.
