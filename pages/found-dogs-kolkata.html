Make the found-dog report useful

Location
Exact neighbourhood, road, park, crossing or landmark.
Time
When and where the dog was first noticed.
Appearance
Size, coat colour, breed/type, sex and visible markings.
Photo
Clear face, ears and body photo from a safe distance.

Protect unique identifiers

Do not publish every distinctive detail. Keeping one or two identifiers private can help you verify that a claimant is the genuine owner.

Keep the dog safe

If safe to do so, provide water and a calm temporary space. Avoid approaching an unfamiliar or frightened dog aggressively. Contact a suitable local rescuer or veterinary professional when needed.

Owner verification

Before handover, ask for older photos, vaccination information or another matching detail that was not publicly disclosed. Meet safely and avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.

FAQs

What location should I record?

Enter Kolkata as the city and provide the exact locality and landmark separately.

Can I hide some identifying details?

Yes. Keeping a distinctive feature private can help with ownership verification.

Can AI Search help?

Yes. A clear photo can be used with FindMyPet India's AI Search to compare available reports.
