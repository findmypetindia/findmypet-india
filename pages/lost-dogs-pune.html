Report a Lost Dog

What to include in a Pune lost-dog report

Give the report enough information for someone in Pune to recognise the dog quickly. Include the last-seen area or landmark, date and approximate time, breed or mix, coat colour and distinctive markings, gender, approximate age, collar details and a clear recent photo.

Where to search

Start with the exact last-seen area and nearby streets, parks, markets, residential lanes and familiar walking routes. Check locations around the last known point first, then expand the search radius. Keep the area and landmark details consistent when sharing the report.

Make the photo useful

Use a clear photo showing the dog's face, ears and distinctive markings. If you have more than one useful photo, keep the clearest identification image available for matching.

Use FindMyPet India's search tools

Search community reports using details such as city, breed and colour, and use the platform's AI-powered photo search when a suitable photo is available.

If you find a possible match

Compare several identifying features rather than relying on colour alone. Check markings, face shape, ears, collar and the reported location/time. Use the contact route provided through the FindMyPet India report rather than publishing private contact details publicly.

Quick checklist

  • Last-seen area and landmark
  • Date and approximate time
  • Clear face and body photo
  • Breed, colour and distinctive markings
  • Gender and approximate age
  • Collar, tag or other identifying details
