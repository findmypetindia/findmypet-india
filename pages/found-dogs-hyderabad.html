What to include in a found-dog report

  • Recent, clear photos showing the dog's face, ears and distinctive markings
  • Color, approximate breed/type, size and identifying marks
  • Found date and approximate time
  • Precise Hyderabad area, street or nearby landmark
  • Only factual details that help a potential owner identify the dog

Found a dog in Hyderabad? What to do next

Keep the dog safe and avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly. Record the exact place and time where you found the dog, check nearby lost-pet reports, and update the report if the dog moves to another safe location. Clear photos can make identification easier.

If the dog appears injured, aggressive or in immediate danger, prioritise appropriate local veterinary or animal-rescue assistance rather than attempting a risky rescue yourself.

Why location details matter

Hyderabad is large, so a useful found-pet report should name the locality plus a nearby recognisable landmark where possible. This gives families a practical search area without publishing unnecessary private information.

FAQs

Can I report a dog I found without knowing its breed?

Yes. A clear photo, color, approximate size, markings and found location can still help identify the dog.

Should I publish my phone number on the page?

No. Use the website's account and contact workflow instead of putting personal phone numbers in public posts.

Can AI Search help identify a found dog?

Yes. You can use the AI Search feature to compare a pet photo with available reports when the feature is available.
