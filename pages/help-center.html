Help Center

Quick answers for reporting, searching and helping pets return home safely.

How do I report a lost pet?

Open Lost Pets, fill the pet details, last seen location and upload a clear recent photo.

How do I report a found pet?

Open Found Pets and add the pet photo, location and any identifying details.

How does AI Search work?

Upload a clear photo. The platform compares it with reports submitted by the community to show possible matches.

Can I edit my report?

Yes. Go to My Dashboard, then select Edit Report. You can also mark a pet as reunited.

Need more help?

For a technical problem, broken link, or urgent correction, send us an email with a screenshot and page name.
