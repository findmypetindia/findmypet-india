Found Dogs in Mumbai

If you find a dog in Mumbai, record the exact locality, landmark, date and time and upload a clear photo so a possible owner can recognise the pet.

Record the exact found location

Include the locality, road, building or landmark where the dog was found and when you saw it. Accurate location and timing can help connect the report with a missing-dog search.

Use a clear photo

Upload a recent photo that shows the dog's face, coat and visible markings. Avoid publishing unnecessary personal information and keep the report focused on identifying the animal.

Verify ownership before handover

Ask a claimant for older photos or private identifying details that were not publicly posted. Compare the information before handing over the dog.
