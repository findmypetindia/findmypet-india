What to include in a found-dog report

  • Clear photos of the dog's face, body and distinctive markings.
  • Exact Jaipur neighbourhood, road or landmark where it was found.
  • Date and approximate time found.
  • Colour, breed or likely breed, collar and visible identifying features.

Help the owner verify the dog

Keep some unique identifying details private where practical. Ask a claimant for reasonable proof such as older photographs, distinctive marks or vaccination information before arranging a handover.
