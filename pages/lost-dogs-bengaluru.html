Help find a lost dog in Bengaluru

If a dog is missing, record the last-seen location as precisely as possible. Add the dog's name, breed, color, gender, age, date/time, distinctive markings and a clear recent photo. Nearby landmarks and the direction the dog was last seen can also make a report more useful.

Useful details

Breed, coat color, size, gender, age, collar or harness, distinctive marks and a recent photo.

Location details

Include the Bengaluru area, nearby landmark, last-seen date/time and any direction of travel you know.

Search for matches

Check existing FindMyPet India reports and use AI-powered photo search to compare possible matches.

Before publishing the report

Check the photo and identifying details carefully, avoid publishing unnecessary personal contact information, and use the website's contact flow when reaching out about a possible match.

Common questions

What should I include in a Bengaluru lost-dog report?

Last-seen area, date/time, breed or appearance, color, age, gender, distinctive markings and a clear photo.

Can I search by photo?

Yes. FindMyPet India's AI Search can help you look for visually similar pet reports.

Should I include my phone number publicly?

No. Share only information needed for identification and use the platform's contact process where available.
