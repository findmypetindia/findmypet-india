Keep Delhi NCR location fields separate

Delhi NCR includes several distinct cities and jurisdictions. If a pet was last seen in Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad, Faridabad or Delhi, select the actual city rather than using “Delhi” as a substitute. Add the sector, colony, block, market, park or gate name and one recognisable landmark. If the search crosses a nearby border, record each confirmed sighting with its own date, time and exact locality instead of combining different locations into one uncertain point.

For example, a report around Dwarka, Rohini or Lajpat Nagar should identify the relevant locality and nearby landmark. A report from Noida or Gurugram should name that city and its sector or neighbourhood. These are examples of location detail, not confirmed sightings or rescue locations.

A practical first-day search plan

  • Save the last-seen time, direction of travel and a clear recent photograph.
  • Ask nearby residents, guards, shopkeepers and regular feeders to check the immediate area.
  • Share one consistent report link so updates do not split across conflicting posts.
  • Record each new sighting separately and check whether it is recent before travelling.

Do not enter roads, metro or railway property, construction sites or restricted areas to search. For a found animal, keep one or two identifying details private and ask a claimant for older photographs or veterinary records before handover.
