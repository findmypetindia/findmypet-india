Lost Dogs in Delhi NCR

If your dog is missing in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad or Ghaziabad, create a clear report with a recent photo and precise last-seen location.

What to include in a lost dog report

  1. Use a recent, clear photo showing the dog's face and distinctive markings.
  2. Enter the correct NCR city and add the sector, colony, road or landmark.
  3. Record the last-seen date, approximate time, collar details and identifying marks.
  4. Share the report link consistently with nearby residents, feeders, vets and rescuers.

Search around the last-seen location

Check familiar walking routes, parks, markets, parking areas and nearby streets first. Repeat the search at quieter times and gradually widen the area.

Check found-pet reports

A found dog may be reported by someone in a nearby locality. Compare photos, coat patterns and distinctive marks rather than relying only on breed or colour.
