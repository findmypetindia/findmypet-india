Add a precise Kolkata location

Choose Kolkata as the city, then enter the specific neighbourhood, street and a recognisable landmark. For example, a report from Salt Lake should name the relevant sector or block; a report from Behala, Jadavpur, Tollygunge, Dum Dum, Garia, New Town or Park Street should include the nearest road, crossing, park or building landmark. These examples are prompts for accurate descriptions, not separate service-area boundaries.

Also record the date and approximate time the pet was last seen. If the animal was walking or running, mention its direction of travel. Avoid sharing a private home address publicly.

What to include in a lost-pet report

  • A recent, well-lit photograph showing the pet clearly.
  • Species, approximate size, coat colour, distinctive markings and collar or harness details.
  • The exact last-seen locality, nearby landmark, date and approximate time.
  • Any safe-to-share contact method and a short note about the pet's temperament or medical needs.

If you receive a possible sighting, confirm its time and exact location before changing the search area. Keep one current report link so that neighbours are not circulating conflicting details.

If you have found a pet in Kolkata

Do not chase or corner a frightened animal. If it is safe, keep a calm distance, offer water and contact a local animal-rescue professional when the animal is injured or distressed. Note where and when it was found and upload a clear photograph with your found-pet report.

For the animal's safety, do not publish every unique identifying feature. Ask anyone claiming the pet to share older photographs, veterinary records or specific details that can establish ownership before arranging a handover.

Use online reporting alongside local searching

Check the last-seen area and nearby familiar routes at different times of day. Where appropriate, inform local veterinary clinics, animal feeders, building security staff and neighbourhood groups. Share the same report link and update it when a sighting is confirmed, rather than creating multiple reports for the same incident.

Frequently asked questions

What location details should I add to a Kolkata pet report?

Add the neighbourhood or locality, nearest road or landmark, date and approximate time. If the pet was moving, include its direction and any later verified sighting.

What should I do if I find a lost pet?

Keep the animal safe if possible, note the exact location and time, take a clear photo from a safe distance and submit a found-pet report. Verify a claimant before handover.

How can I help search for my missing pet?

Create a report with a recent photo and precise last-seen location. Recheck nearby streets and familiar routes, contact local vets and animal feeders, and update the report when a sighting is verified.
