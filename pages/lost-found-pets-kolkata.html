Make the location precise

Use Kolkata as the city and add the neighbourhood, road, crossing, park or landmark separately. Precise local details are especially important in busy urban areas where a pet can move between adjacent neighbourhoods quickly.

Combine online and ground search

Check the last-seen area repeatedly, speak with local feeders, security guards, vets and animal rescuers, and share a single current photo and report link in neighbourhood groups. Update the report if a credible sighting moves the search area.

Verify ownership before handover

For a found pet, avoid publishing every unique identifying detail. Keep one or two identifiers private and ask a claimant to provide older photos or matching information before handover.
