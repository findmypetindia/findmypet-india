What to include in a lost-dog report

  • A recent, clear photo showing the face, coat and distinctive markings.
  • Dog's name, breed or type, color, gender, age and identifying marks.
  • Last-seen date, approximate time, Hyderabad area, nearby landmark and direction of travel.
  • Any collar, tag, harness or other visible identifier.
  • Contact information through your FindMyPet India account rather than posting a personal phone number publicly.

How to search for a lost dog in Hyderabad

Start with the exact area where the dog was last seen, then check nearby community reports because a frightened or displaced dog can move beyond the original location. Compare coat color, face markings, ears, size and other distinctive features rather than relying only on breed.

Use AI Search for photo-based discovery where appropriate, and review possible matches carefully before contacting a reporter.

Quick checklist

Save the original photo, note the last-seen location and time, check nearby reports regularly, and update your report if the dog is found. If you spot a potentially matching dog, use the platform's available reporting/contact flow instead of publishing someone's private details.
