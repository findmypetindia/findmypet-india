Report a Found CatSearch Pets

What to include in a found-cat report

  • Clear photos showing the face, coat pattern and distinctive marks.
  • Exact Jaipur neighbourhood, road or landmark where the cat was found.
  • Date and approximate time found.
  • Colour, breed or likely breed, eye colour, collar and other identifying features.

Help the family verify ownership

Keep some unique identifying details private where practical. Ask a claimant for reasonable proof such as older photographs, distinctive marks or vaccination information before arranging a handover.
