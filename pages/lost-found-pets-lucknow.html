Create a clear lost or found pet report in Lucknow

Give the specific locality and a nearby landmark in Lucknow so that readers can understand the search area. If you know the route or direction in which a missing pet moved, include it as an observation rather than a certainty.

  • Include the last-seen or found date, approximate time and locality.
  • Add a recent photo and describe colour, size, coat pattern, collar and unique markings.
  • For a found animal, mention its visible condition and whether it needs immediate assistance.
  • Keep your contact details current, but do not post your full home address publicly.
  • Be alert to fake recovery requests; do not share OTPs, passwords or payment credentials.

Frequently asked questions

How can I report a lost pet in Lucknow?

Sign in and complete the lost-pet form with a precise locality, nearby landmark, date, photograph and identifying details.

How do I report a pet I have found?

Sign in and choose the found-pet report option. Add the location where the animal was seen and a clear description.

Are suggested photo matches reliable proof?

No. AI/photo matching only suggests possible matches. Confirm identity using distinctive features and direct communication with the submitter.

Is this a 24-hour animal rescue helpline?

No. FindMyPet India is a community reporting platform, not an emergency helpline or dispatch service. Contact an appropriate local animal-welfare or emergency service directly when immediate intervention is needed.
