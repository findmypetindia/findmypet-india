What to include in a Pune pet report

For a useful local search, mention the Pune neighbourhood and the nearest recognisable landmark rather than writing only “Pune”. Areas such as Baner, Aundh, Kothrud, Hadapsar, Wakad, Hinjawadi, Viman Nagar and Kondhwa can help readers understand the search area when relevant to your case.

  • Add the date and approximate time the pet was last seen or found.
  • Include a recent, clear photograph and describe coat colour, size, collar, markings and other identifying features.
  • For a lost pet, mention the last-seen location and the direction the pet may have moved, if known.
  • For a found pet, describe where the animal was seen and whether it appears injured or needs urgent help.
  • Keep one contact method active and be cautious of people demanding money or verification codes.

Frequently asked questions

How can I report a lost pet in Pune?

Sign in to FindMyPet India and use the lost-pet report form. Add the neighbourhood, landmark, date, clear photo and identifying details.

Can I report a pet found in my area?

Yes. Sign in and submit a found-pet report with the exact area, a safe-to-take photograph and a description. Do not put yourself or the animal at risk to take a photo.

Does an AI match prove that this is my pet?

No. Photo or AI results are possible matches only. Compare distinctive markings and other details, and confirm directly with the report submitter before taking action.

Is FindMyPet India an emergency rescue service?

No. It is a community reporting and discovery platform, not an emergency dispatch service. If an animal is in immediate danger, contact an appropriate local animal-welfare or emergency service directly.
