What to include in a Kolkata lost-dog report

Location
Neighbourhood, road, crossing, park, building or landmark.
Timing
Last-seen date, approximate time and direction of travel.
Appearance
Breed/type, coat colour, size, sex and distinctive markings.
Photo
Use a recent, clear image showing the dog's face, ears and body.

Search beyond the exact last-seen point

Check nearby lanes, parks, markets, building compounds and familiar walking routes. Ask security guards, local feeders, vets and rescuers to watch for the dog. If a credible sighting changes the search area, update the report.

Use one clear identifying photo

Avoid flooding groups with many versions. A clear photo plus the report link makes the information easier to recognise and share.

Safety and privacy

Do not publish unnecessary personal information. Arrange handovers in a safe public setting and verify credible sightings before travelling or paying anyone.

FAQs

What location should I enter?

Enter Kolkata as the city and provide the specific locality, road or landmark in the relevant area field.

Can I search by photo?

Yes. Use FindMyPet India's AI Search to help compare a pet photo with available reports.

What if my dog was seen somewhere else?

Update the report with the new sighting, time and exact location so the search trail stays useful.
