What Is FindMyPet India?

FindMyPet India helps people report lost pets, report found pets, search community reports and review AI-assisted pet-photo matches across India.

FindMyPet India is an India-focused platform for lost & found pet reporting, community search and AI-assisted photo matching. It brings pet reports, location details and photo-based comparison together to help people review possible matches and reconnect pets with their families.

What FindMyPet India helps you do

✓ Report & Search

  • Create lost pet reports
  • Create found pet reports
  • Search community pet reports
  • Add city, state, locality and last-seen details

✓ AI-Assisted Matching

  • Compare pet photos with available reports
  • Review possible visual similarities
  • Use location, dates and markings alongside photo matches
  • Verify ownership before any handover

How it can help with a lost pet

A pet parent can create a report with a clear photo, last-seen location, city, state, date and identifying details. They can also review community reports and use AI-assisted photo search to look for possible visual similarities.

How it can help with a found pet

A person who finds a dog, cat or other pet can create a found-pet report with a photo and location details. A possible owner can compare the report with their missing pet and verify ownership using additional evidence.

What AI-assisted pet search means

AI-assisted search compares visible characteristics in pet photos with available reports to surface possible similarities. It is a discovery aid, so location, dates, markings and ownership evidence should also be checked before confirming a match.
