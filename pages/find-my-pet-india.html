What to do when your pet is missing

Act quickly and use more than one channel. Create a detailed online report, search the nearby area, contact local animal rescuers or NGOs, and share a clear recent photo with the exact last-seen location.

  • Use a recent, clear front-face or full-body photo.
  • Add city, state, area or landmark and the date the pet went missing.
  • Mention collar colour, marks, behaviour or other useful identifiers.
  • Keep checking new found-pet reports for possible matches.

What to do when you find a pet

Photograph the animal safely, note the exact area and time, and avoid making assumptions about ownership. A detailed found-pet report can help the family identify the pet quickly.

Safety tip: Before handing a found pet to someone claiming ownership, ask for reasonable proof such as older photos, identifying marks, vaccination details or other information that is not visible in the public report.
