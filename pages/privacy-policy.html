🔒 PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Information we collect

When you create an account or submit a pet report, we collect only the details you provide: name, email address, contact number, pet details, report location and the pet photo you choose.

What we do not ask for

FindMyPet India does not ask for OTPs, payment details, phone contacts, or access to your full photo gallery. You choose the pet photo yourself. The website does not use advertising or Google Analytics tracking.

How we use it

This information is used only to operate FindMyPet India, display pet reports, enable contact between people involved in a report and protect against misuse.

Public information

Pet details, photos, report locations and Call or WhatsApp contact buttons may be visible with a report so the community can help. Do not submit information you do not want associated with a public pet report.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect platform data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can request changes or deletion of your own report through My Dashboard or by emailing us.
