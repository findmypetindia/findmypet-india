🔒 PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Information we collect

When you create an account or submit a pet report, we may collect your name, email address, contact number, pet details, report location and photo.

How we use it

This information is used to operate FindMyPet India, display pet reports, enable contact between people involved in a report, improve the platform and protect against misuse.

Public information

Pet details, photos and report locations may be visible to visitors so the community can help. Contact options are hidden by default and shown only after a visitor chooses to reveal them. Do not submit information you do not want associated with a public pet report.

Photo privacy

New report photos are re-saved before upload to remove common camera metadata. Do not include a full home address, passwords, OTPs, bank details or government-ID details in a report.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect platform data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can request changes or deletion of your own report through My Dashboard or by emailing us.
