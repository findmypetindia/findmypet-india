🔒 PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FindMyPet India handles information when you visit the website, create an account, submit a pet or animal-rescue report, or contact us.

Information you provide

Depending on the feature you use, information may include your name, email address, mobile or WhatsApp number, account details, pet details, report description, city, state, area, location, photographs, videos and information you submit in a rescue-partner application.

How information is used

We use submitted information to operate user accounts, publish and manage reports, help users review possible lost-and-found matches, support communication about a report, process rescue-partner applications, respond to support requests and protect the platform against misuse.

Public reports and contact details

Information submitted for a community report may be displayed to other visitors or signed-in users, depending on the feature and the report settings. A report may include an animal photograph, description and location. Contact details may be made available through report contact options. Please submit only information you are comfortable sharing for the purpose of helping an animal.

Service providers and storage

FindMyPet India uses Supabase services for account authentication, database and file-storage features. Information needed to provide these features may be processed by the service provider on our behalf, subject to its own terms and privacy practices. Website hosting and delivery providers may also process basic technical information, such as IP address, browser details and server logs, to deliver and secure the website.

Cookies, local storage and sign-in

The website and its authentication services may use browser storage or similar technologies to maintain sign-in sessions, remember necessary settings and support security. You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings; disabling necessary storage may affect sign-in or website functionality.

Advertising and Google AdSense

FindMyPet India may display advertisements from Google AdSense or other advertising partners if advertising is enabled on the website. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertisements, subject to the choices and controls available in your region. These technologies may use information such as browser identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and visits to this or other websites.

When Google advertising is used, Google may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on a user's visits to this website and other websites. Visitors can learn how Google uses information from partner sites at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and manage relevant ad-personalisation settings through Google My Ad Center. The availability of controls may vary by location and device.

Advertising may not yet be active on every page. This section describes the practices that apply if and when advertising technologies are enabled.

Data retention, security and your choices

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the platform, maintain reports and accounts, meet legitimate operational requirements, resolve disputes and address misuse. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have submitted, subject to applicable requirements and the need to retain certain records. You can also use available account or dashboard features to manage your reports.

Children

FindMyPet India is intended for people who can lawfully use an online account and submit reports. Children should use the platform only with appropriate parent or guardian guidance.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website's features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email indiafindmypet@gmail.com or visit our Contact Us page.
