Found Cats in Delhi NCR

If you found a cat in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad or Ghaziabad, report the exact location and a clear photo to help the owner find it.

Create a useful found-cat report

  1. Take a clear photo showing the face, coat pattern and distinctive features.
  2. Enter the correct NCR city and add the locality, sector, colony, road or landmark.
  3. Record the date and approximate time the cat was found.
  4. Keep public information focused on identification and avoid unnecessary personal information.

Verify ownership before handover

Ask a claimant for reasonable proof such as older photographs, distinctive markings or vaccination information. Avoid publishing every identifying detail so genuine owners can demonstrate knowledge of them.

Compare nearby lost-cat reports

Check lost reports from the surrounding NCR area and compare the actual facial markings, coat pattern and other distinctive features.
