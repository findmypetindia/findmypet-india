Help find a lost cat in Bengaluru

When reporting a missing cat, include the cat's name, breed or type, coat color and pattern, gender, approximate age, last-seen area, date and nearby landmarks. A clear recent photo can make identification easier.

For indoor cats, also mention whether the cat may be hiding nearby and any distinctive markings such as facial patterns, ear shape, tail markings, collar or other identifying features.

What to include in your report

  • Exact or nearby last-seen area and recognizable landmark.
  • Date and approximate time the cat was last seen.
  • Coat color, pattern, breed/type and distinctive markings.
  • Recent clear photographs from useful angles.
  • Any collar or other non-sensitive identifying details.

FAQs

What location should I enter?

Use the last-seen area as precisely as you can, along with a nearby landmark. Avoid publishing unnecessary private residential details.

Can I report a cat without knowing the breed?

Yes. Color, coat pattern, distinctive markings, approximate age and a clear photo can still help identification.

Should I publish my phone number on the page?

No. Use the platform's available contact flow and avoid putting sensitive contact information in public descriptions.
