THE CASE STUDY

From scattered social posts to a clearer reunion journey.

The problem

Missing-pet information is usually spread across WhatsApp groups and social media. Reports can be incomplete, difficult to search and slow to reach the people who may be able to help.

The solution

FindMyPet India brings reports, photos, location details and direct contact actions into one structured, responsive experience for people who have lost or found a pet.

Product principle

Keep reporting free, easy to understand and trustworthy during an emotional moment for pet owners.

WHAT I BUILT

Product capabilities that make reports useful.

The product combines a clear reporting flow with live data, mobile usability and trust-building support content.

01

Structured reporting

Multi-step lost and found forms collect pet, owner, location and photo details in a clear order.

02

Secure accounts

Supabase Authentication supports signup, login, password recovery and protected user sessions.

03

Live report data

Supabase PostgreSQL and Storage make reports and submitted pet images available as live application data.

04

Mobile-first experience

Responsive layouts, an accessible navigation drawer and PWA support keep the product practical on phones.

05

Image-search foundation

A photo-comparison workflow is in place as a foundation for future machine-learning image matching.

06

Trust & support

Help, contact, privacy, terms and report-issue pages make the platform more transparent for users and NGOs.

SELECTED DECISIONS

Small engineering choices with visible user impact.

Data-first report pages

Lists and pet-detail pages read from Supabase so new reports can appear without rebuilding the interface.

Resilient mobile navigation

A responsive drawer and overlay prevent desktop links from disappearing on smaller screens.

Fresh releases for visitors

Versioned assets and service-worker cache updates reduce the chance of users receiving older code after a release.
