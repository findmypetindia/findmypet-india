# FindMyPet India

> A mobile-first lost-and-found pet reporting platform built to help families and communities reconnect pets with their homes.

[Visit the live project](https://findmypet-india.vercel.app/) · [View the project portfolio](https://findmypet-india.vercel.app/portfolio.html)

## The problem

When a pet goes missing, information is often scattered across WhatsApp groups and social media posts. Important details can be incomplete, hard to search and slow to reach people nearby who may be able to help.

## The solution

FindMyPet India provides one clear place to report a lost or found pet, share photos and location details, browse pet reports and contact the relevant person.

## Key features

- Lost and found pet reporting flows
- Pet photos, identification details, reward and last-seen location
- Searchable pet report listings and individual detail pages
- User signup, login, password recovery and protected sessions
- Direct Call and WhatsApp contact actions
- Responsive mobile navigation and Progressive Web App support
- Help Center, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions and Report Issue pages
- A photo-comparison workflow designed as a foundation for future ML image matching
